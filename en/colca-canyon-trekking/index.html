Colca Canyon Trekking

City

AREQUIPA

Life Experience

ENJOY IT WITH YOUR FAMILY OR FRIENDS

Duration

03 DAYS - 02 NIGHTS

Itinerary

Day 01 - Arequipa / Cabanaconde / Chivay / Colca Canyon (L, D)

Before sunrise, pick up from the hotel at approximately 03:00 a.m., we will move to Cabanaconde Town, during the way we will make a stop at Cruz del Cóndor Viewpoint to appreciate the beautiful landscape of Colca Canyon. We continue to the town of Chivay to enjoy lunch.

Before sunrise, pick up from the hotel at approximately 03:00 a.m., we will move to Cabanaconde Town, during the way we will make a stop at Cruz del Cóndor Viewpoint to appreciate the beautiful landscape of Colca Canyon. We continue to the town of Chivay to enjoy lunch.

Day 02 - San Juan De Chuccho / Coshñirhua / Oasis (B, L, D)

Early in the morning we will have breakfast and continue with our second day of walking. During this trip we will visit the different villages, on the right bank side of the Canyon such as Tapay and Malata which has its own onsite Museum. Then, we will go to  Coshñirhua to spend a pleasant time with the inhabitants talking about their experiences and customs. Then, we will arrive at Oasis, where we can enjoy its swimming pools and the beautiful natural landscape of the depth of the Canyon. Accommodation and rest in bungalows. Overnight.

Day 03 - Cabanaconde / Chivay / Arequipa (B)

Early in the morning we start walking up towards the village of Cabanaconde. There we will wait for our vehicle to take us back to Arequipa. During the way,  our last stop will be Chivay for lunch. Return to Arequipa.

Optional.- Visit to the Thermal Baths

B= Breakfast,  L= Lunch,  D= Dinner
