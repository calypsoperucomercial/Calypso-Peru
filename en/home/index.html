Machu Picchu
Fortress of Kuelap
An important pre-Inca archaeological site located in the northeastern Andes of Peru, in the Province of Luya and it was built by the Chachapoyas. It forms a large stone architectural characterized by its monumental condition, with a large artificial platform, built on a limestone rock ridge at the top of Barreta Hill (at 3,000 m.a.s.l)

Gocta
Amazing waterfall, which measures 771m and it is considered N° 15 in the world ranking. Locally known like “La Chocarrera”, it is divided in 2 sections: the first one is 231 m and the second is 540 m. You can reach it in approx. 2 Hrs. 30 min on foot.

Inca Trail
It is considered the best trekking in South America because of its historical and adventure value. The Inca Trail belongs to the Inca Roads system (30,000 km) that gives us the meaning of its history through archaeological remains along the way until we discover the magic of "Machu Picchu"

Titicaca Lake
It is the highest navigable body of water, located in the Andean Highlands at an altitude average of 3,812 m.s.n.m, between Perú (56%) and Bolivia (44%). It was populated by numerous Andean cultures that persist to this day on its beautiful islands.
See more
Chan Chan
The largest adobe citadel in America and the second largest in the world. It is pre-Columbian citadel made of adobe built on the coast of Peru by the Chimus. It is located in the region of La Libertad, between Trujillo and Huanchaco cities. In 1986 was declared Cultural World Heritage by Unesco and included on the List of World Heritage in Danger in the same year. It is divided into nine 'citadels' or 'palaces' forming autonomous units.

Cruises
Crossing the incredible Amazon River aboard a luxurious cruise. This experience give us a unique opportunity to observe the Amazon wildlife like howler monkeys, macaws and pink dolphins under warm weather. Photograph a three-toed sloth while it hangs from a nearby branch

Manu
Protected natural space located in the Southeast of Peru, between the department of Madre de Dios and Cusco. It has an area of 1 909 800 hectares and is a Natural Heritage of Humanity because of its outstanding universal value and mega-diversity, becoming it one of the most important areas on the planet.

Huascaran
It is the highest snow-capped mountain in Peru with 6,768 m. Located in the White Mountain Range, it is the most popular snow-capped mountain and every year thousands of mountaineers come to challenge its imposing peaks. It has 2 summits, between them highlight the south part
See more
Caral
The oldest citadel of Peru (more than 500 years from now) and the first Andean civilization place to develop an own and singular social organization. Caral developed at the same time as other great cultures in Mesopotamia, Egypt and China. Its pyramids are the oldest found in the Andes until now
