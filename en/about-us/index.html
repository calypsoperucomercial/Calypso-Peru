ABOUT US

Providing tailor-made experiences to each of our clients

We were born as a Travel agency in 1994 under the name of Calypso Travel, we evolved quickly with the name of CALYPSO REPS S.A.C and we became the Leading Agency, wholesaler of domestic tourism

Today, CALYPSO PERÚ, is an Travel Agency recognized as an important Tour Operator which promotes Peru as a tourist destination in its different natural regions: sea, coast, mountains, high and low jungle

We have highly qualified professionals to provide “tailored” solutions to our clients’ requirements and provide them a quality-personalized attention.
What we provide?

Design a product or service that meets customer expectations; for this reason, the most important thing is to know the passenger in his/her human dimension and identify their main travel motivations, emotional and rational, helping them in the purchase decision of selected product.
To be the leading tour operator with a competitive offer that is respectful of culture and nature. Recognized for the professionalism and security that we provide to our clients, who receive from our company a unique attention, making them feel special.

¿Our services ?

We provide consulting, trading and operation of business or leisure trips activities, providing the best and most efficient service for each of our individual passengers and groups
