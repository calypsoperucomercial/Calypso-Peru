The Hidden Treasures In Amazonas

Location

AMAZONAS

Life Experience

ENJOY IT WITH YOUR FAMILY OR FRIENDS

Duration

04 DAYS - 03 NIGHTS

Itinerary

Day 01 - Chachapoyas In

Reception and transfer from Chachapoyas Airport to the hotel.  Free time, night off. Overnight.

NOTE: ROUTES TO GO TO CHACHAPOYAS CITY

  • Due to the flight frequency and airlines available from Lima to Jaén, transfers start from that airpot. The approximate time from Shumba Airport (Jaén) to Chachapoyas is 4 hours. During the route we will enjoy an interesting landscape, crossing Marañón River and Bagua and Pedro Ruiz villages reaching the city of Chachapoyas.
  • In case the PAX prefers a direct flight, so far only one airline offers this service with departures and returns on Mondays and Fridays between 09:00 – 11:30 Hrs. Travel time from Chachapoyas Airport to the City of Chachapoyas is approximately 17 minutes.
Day 02 - Karajía Sarcophagi + Quiocta Carverns (B, L)

After breakfast, we will start our tour at 08.00 am. heading towards Cruz Pata Village, where our adventure starts with a short walk to the burial site of Karajía Sarcophagi, which houses a set of funeral statues, located in an inaccessible rocky cliff. This form of burial is considered unique in the world because it is made of stone, wood and clay. After contemplate this impressive and unique Chachapoyas burial structures, we will return for lunch to the city of Luya. We will continue visiting Quiocta Caverns, where we can see thousand stalagmites and bizarre forms of the limestone sculptures in different sizes; along the way we can see skeletons scattered throughout all environments. The ancient Chachapoyas used this place as a cemetery. Return to Chachapoyas, free time, overnight.

Day 03 - Catarata De Gocta (B, L)

Breakfast at the hotel. At the indicated time we will go to Cocachimba Town, 1 hour distance from Chachapoyas. From there we will start a beautiful hike about two hours crossing a mountain landscapes with a huge variety of flora and fauna; endemic species such as yellow-tailed woolly monkey, Gallito de las Rocas, spoonbill hummingbird, among others until reaching Gocta Waterfall. This beautiful waterfall, discovered in 2002 by the German Stefan Ziemendorff, was once considered the third highest waterfall in the world at 771m high, today it is the fifteenth. We will arrive at Gocta Waterfall where we will enjoy an impressive natural show. At the appropriate time we will Cocachimba Town (2h approx.) to taste a typical lunch. In the afternoon come back the City of Chachapoyas. Free time. Overnight.

NOTE: This excursion is on foot or riding a mule.

Day 04 - Kuelap Archaeological Complex + Macro (B, L)

We will have an early breakfast at the hotel and then start our tour to Kuélap Archeological Zone, located 70 km west of Chachapoyas and surrounding by high walls of Chachapoyas culture. This fortress is unique in the world because of location on top of a mountain with three precipices on its four sides, vegetation and elevated outcrops or cliffs. Built in an area of ​​beautiful landscapes between the Andes and the Amazon jungle, creating an incomparable spectacle. In the complex there are more than 400 circular buildings along ​​6 hectares, with a remarkable decoration as “El Castillo”, “El Tintero” and el complejo “Pueblo Alto“, this impressive place was declared a Cultural Heritage of Peru in 1998.  Our first stop will be Macro Archeological zone to have a brief speech. Then continue the trip to Nuevo Tingo town and boarding the cable car, after 20 minutes in the cable cars, enjoying the scenery, we will arrive at La Malca from where we will walk 20 minutes to the fortress. Together with our professional guide we will visit different places such as: ceremonial sites, the main temple, circular rooms, etc. Return in cable cars to Poblado de Nuevo Tingo where a delicious lunch will be waiting for us. At the appropriate time, transfer to the city of Chachapoyas. Free time. Overnight.

Day 05 - Revash Mausoleums + Museum Of Leymebamba (B, L)

 After breakfast, we will go to Revash Mausoleums, which are high valuable rectangular constructions homes with 1, 2 and 3 floors. The majority are “funeral homes”, they are painted in red and cream color, which resemble small houses and in some cases it is possible see primitive designs of animals and people. Then, we will go to Leymebamba village to have lunch, and after that visit the museum of Centro Mallqui which design replicates local architectural traditions. This museum holds more than 2000 valuables items, 219 mummies and an important collection of Quipus, an informative storage instrument used in Andean civilizations; all of them found in the  Laguna de los Cóndores (Lagoon of the Condors). The museum also exhibits ceramic, textiles and weapons from ancient Peruvian cultures. Return to the city of Chachapoyas. Free time. Overnight

Day 06 - Chachapoyas Out (B)

Desayuno en el hotel. Tiempo libre para realizar actividades personales. A la hora oportuna traslado al Aeropuerto de Jaén o Estación de bus.
